This week I mastered binary search trees. A BST is a tree where every

children are larger. This simple rule makes searching incredibly fast - 
you can eliminate half the remaining data with each comparison. It's 
like looking up a word in a dictionary by always opening to the middle 
and deciding left or right. BSTs are perfect for maintaining sorted data
