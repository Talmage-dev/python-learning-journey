## 🎯 Core Principles (Unchanged)

### The Foundation:
1. **Gain skills and KEEP them** - Deep understanding and long-term retention
2. **Complete all roadmap goals** - MIT-level CS knowledge
3. **Timeline is flexible** - 1 year, 3 years, 5 years - mastery matters, not speed
4. **Enjoy the process** - Sustainable learning, no burnout
5. **YOUR honest assessment determines progress** - No fake checkboxes! ← NEW!

### The Basketball Model:
Just like learning to dribble a basketball:
- **Learn** the technique (understanding)
- **Practice daily** until automatic (intensive practice)
- **Master** it completely (90%+ mastery AND you feel confident)
- **Maintain forever** (keep skills sharp)

Once you can dribble, you never forget - but you still practice to stay sharp.

## 📅 NEW Daily Structure (3.5-5 hours)

### **Morning Session (90 min)**

#### 1. Quick Review (5 min)
- Review today's objectives (created last night)
- Mental preparation
- Know your focus for the day

#### 2. Typing Practice (15 min)
- Keybr.com for focused practice
- Track progress weekly (not daily - avoid micro-stress)
- Goal: 40+ WPM by end of Year 1
- Current: ~33 WPM average

**Why:** Typing speed impacts everything. 15 min/day compounds to fluency.

---

#### 3. **"Challenge Mode" Maintenance (30 min)** ← NEW!

**Two Types of Maintenance:**

**Type A: "Needs Review" Topics (25-30 min)**
- Topics you're confident with BUT need review to feel automatic
- Examples: Selection Sort, Insertion Sort, Bubble Sort, Both Linked Lists
- **Challenge:** "How many problems can you complete in 30 min?"
- Track: Number completed, accuracy, speed
- Goal: Build speed + confidence through volume
- Frequency: 2-3x per week until YOU feel confident

**Type B: "Truly Solid" Topics (15 min)**
- Topics you're fully confident with
- Examples: Stack, Queue
- Quick refresh only
- 1 problem + complexity check
- Frequency: 1x per week

**Example "Challenge Mode" Session:**
```
Selection Sort (30 min):
- Min 0-5: Implement from memory
- Min 5-10: Problem 1 (sort array)
- Min 10-15: Problem 2 (sort in reverse)
- Min 15-20: Problem 3 (custom comparison)
- Min 20-25: Problem 4 (find kth smallest)
- Min 25-30: Review, check answers

Result: "Completed 4/5 problems, 80% accuracy"
Next time: Aim for 5/5!
```

---

#### 4. Intensive Practice Session 1 (40 min)
- Focus on PRIMARY weak area (currently not confident)
- Deep, focused practice
- Multiple problems/implementations
- This is where skills move from weak to mastered

**Current Primary Focus:** Hash Tables (75-80%)

---

### **Main Session (90-120 min)**

#### 5. Intensive Practice Session 2 (60 min)
- Focus on SECONDARY weak area
- Continue building mastery
- Different problems/approaches

**Current Secondary Focus:** Graphs (82%)

#### 6. Deep Dive / Integration (30-60 min) - OPTIONAL
- Based on energy level
- Integration projects
- Deeper exploration
- Extra practice if needed

---

### **Evening Session (15 min)**

#### 7. Evening Drill (5 min)
- Quick practice of today's main focus
- From memory, no reference materials
- Speed test to confirm retention
- Simple but crucial

**Example:**
- Write one method from memory
- Solve one quick problem
- Confirm understanding

---

#### 8. Daily Review & Planning (10 min)

**Step 1: Score Today (5 min)**
- Rate each objective: ✅ Full (1.0), ⚠️ Partial (0.5), ❌ Not Done (0.0)
- Calculate: Total Points / 5 = Score
- Example: 4.0/5.0 = 80%

**Step 2: Interpret (2 min)**
- 90-100%: 🔥 Excellent - move forward
- 75-89%: ✅ Good - move forward, note weak areas
- 60-74%: ⚠️ Okay - move forward + extra practice
- <60%: 🔄 Focus day needed tomorrow

**Step 3: Create Tomorrow's Objectives (3 min)**
- Based on today's score
- Specific and measurable
- Adjust if needed

**Step 4: Key Insight (1 min)**
- Write 1-2 sentences
- What clicked? What's still fuzzy?
- Builds learning awareness

## 🎯 The Three Practice Phases

### Phase 1: 🔴 Learning (Initial Exposure)

**When:** First time encountering a topic

**Duration:** 60-90 minutes (one focused session)

**Activities:**
- Understand the concept deeply
- Ask "why does this work?" not just "how"
- See multiple examples and use cases
- Initial implementation with reference
- Ask questions, explore, be curious

**Goal:** Build a solid mental model

**Success Indicator:** Can explain the concept in your own words

**Next Step:** Move IMMEDIATELY to Phase 2 next day (no week-long gap!)

---

### Phase 2: 🟡 Intensive Practice (Building Mastery)

**When:** Day after learning, continue until mastery

**Duration:** 40 min + 60 min daily (2 sessions per day)

**Goal:** Move from "I can do this with reference" to "I can do this automatically AND confidently"

**Focus:** MAX 2 topics in intensive practice at any time

---

#### Intensive Practice Activities:

**Session 1 (40 min) - Morning:**
- 3-4 problems/use cases
- Focus on variety
- Build pattern recognition
- Track time and accuracy

**Session 2 (60 min) - Main:**
- 4-5 more problems/use cases
- Deeper exploration
- Edge cases
- Complexity analysis

**Daily Volume:** 7-9 problems/implementations per topic

---

#### Success Criteria (Move to Phase 3 when ALL are true):

✅ **Automatic Recall**
- Can write all methods/algorithms without thinking
- No reference needed at all
- Feels natural and easy

✅ **Confident Problem Solving**
- Solve problems using this concept comfortably
- Know when to apply it
- Understand trade-offs

✅ **Can Teach It**
- Explain clearly to someone else
- Understand not just how but why
- Answer questions about it

✅ **Consistent Performance**
- Daily scores 90%+ for this specific topic
- Multiple days in a row

✅ **YOU Feel Ready** ← MOST IMPORTANT!
- Trust your understanding
- No anxiety about using it
- Feels solid
- **Honest self-assessment says "I'm confident"**

**Timeline:** Usually 1-2 weeks of daily intensive practice

---

### Phase 3: 🟢 Maintenance (Forever)

**When:** After achieving 90%+ mastery AND you feel confident

**Duration:** 15-30 min per topic (rotating schedule)

**Goal:** Never forget - keep skills sharp forever

---

#### Spaced Repetition Schedule:

**Weeks 1-2 (Fresh Mastery):**
- Frequency: 2x per week
- Duration: 15-25 min per session
- Focus: Confirm solid foundation
- Type: "Challenge Mode" if still building speed

**Weeks 3-4 (Establishing):**
- Frequency: 1x per week
- Duration: 15 min per session
- Focus: Maintain automaticity

**Weeks 5-8 (Spacing Out):**
- Frequency: Every 2 weeks
- Duration: 15 min per session
- Focus: Quick refresh

**Week 9+ (Long-term):**
- Frequency: Monthly or before use
- Duration: 15 min per session
- Focus: Stay current

---

#### If Performance Drops:

**Warning Signs:**
- Takes longer than 5 min to implement
- Making errors in basic methods
- Can't solve problems confidently
- YOU don't feel confident anymore

**Action:** Move back to Phase 2 (Intensive Practice) temporarily
- 1 week of daily practice
- Return to maintenance when 90%+ AND confident again
- This is rare if maintenance is consistent!

## 🔄 The Learning → Intensive Flow (NEW!)

### **The Problem We Solved:**
- OLD: Learn topic → wait a week → intensive practice = **FORGETTING GAP**
- NEW: Learn topic → NEXT DAY intensive practice = **RETENTION**

### **The New Flow:**

**Day X: Topic A and Topic B in Intensive Practice**
- Morning: Maintenance + Intensive Session 1 (Topic A)
- Main: Intensive Session 2 (Topic B)
- Both topics getting daily practice

**Day X+1: Topic A reaches 90% + YOU feel confident!** 🎉
- Morning: Maintenance + Intensive Session 1 (Topic B only)
- Main: **LEARN NEW TOPIC C** (60-90 min)
  - Understand deeply
  - Initial implementation
  - Build mental model
- Evening: Drill + Review

**Day X+2: New topic immediately into intensive**
- Morning: Maintenance + Intensive Session 1 (Topic B)
- Main: Intensive Session 2 (**Topic C** - fresh in your mind!)
- Back to 2 topics in intensive
- NO forgetting gap!

**Continue until one reaches 90% + confident...**

---

### **The Cycle:**

```
2 Topics in Intensive
    ↓
One reaches 90% + YOU feel confident
    ↓
Moves to Maintenance (celebrate! 🎉)
    ↓
Learn NEW topic (same day, 60-90 min)
    ↓
New topic → Intensive next day
    ↓
Back to 2 Topics in Intensive
    ↓
Repeat...
```

**This keeps:**
- Focused practice (max 2 intensive topics)
- Immediate reinforcement (no forgetting gap)
- Steady progress (always moving forward)
- Sustainable pace (not overwhelming)

## 📊 Honest Assessment System (NEW!)

### **The Old Way (Didn't Work):**
- Move to maintenance based on performance alone
- Ignore how YOU actually feel
- Tick boxes even if not confident
- Skills fade because foundation wasn't solid

### **The New Way (Works!):**

**After each practice session, I ask YOU:**
1. "How do you feel about this topic?"
2. "Are you confident solving problems with it?"
3. "Can you do this in 5 minutes?"
4. "Do you feel ready for maintenance?"

**We move to maintenance ONLY when:**
- Performance is 90%+ ✅
- YOU honestly feel confident ✅
- Speed is there ✅
- YOU say "I'm ready" ✅

**No more fake progress!** Mastery is the goal, not ticking boxes.

---

### **Honest Status Categories:**

**🟢 TRULY MASTERED:**
- Performance: 90%+
- Feeling: Confident
- Speed: Fast (5 min or less)
- Ready: Yes, feels solid

**🟡 MAINTENANCE - "Needs Review":**
- Performance: 90%+
- Feeling: Confident BUT not quick
- Speed: Slow (needs review to remember)
- Ready: Almost, needs more reps

**🔴 INTENSIVE PRACTICE:**
- Performance: <90%
- Feeling: Not confident
- Speed: Slow or struggling
- Ready: No, needs practice

## 📅 Weekly Review (Sunday Evening, 20 min)

### Purpose:
- Assess week's overall progress
- Update skills status with HONEST assessment
- Review and adjust roadmap
- Plan next week's focus
- Catch issues early

---

### Step 1: Performance Analysis (5 min)

**Calculate Weekly Average:**
```
Mon: 4.5/5 (90%)
Tue: 4.0/5 (80%)
Wed: 4.5/5 (90%)
Thu: 5.0/5 (100%)
Fri: 4.5/5 (90%)
Sat: 4.0/5 (80%)
Sun: 4.5/5 (90%)

Average: 4.43/5 = 88.6% ✅ Excellent week!
```

**Identify Patterns:**
- What consistently went well? (Keep doing!)
- What consistently struggled? (Address next week)
- Any recurring blockers? (Fix these)
- Energy/motivation level? (Sustainability check)

---

### Step 2: Honest Skills Status Update (5 min) ← UPDATED!

**For each skill, ask yourself:**
1. What's my performance level? (%)
2. How do I FEEL about it? (Confident? Need review? Not confident?)
3. How's my speed? (Fast? Slow? Need review?)
4. Am I ready for maintenance? (Honest answer)

**Update categories based on HONEST answers:**

**🟢 Truly Mastered:**
- List each with %
- All feel confident and fast
- Maintenance schedule working

**🟡 Maintenance - "Needs Review":**
- List each with %
- Confident but need review to be fast
- Move to "Challenge Mode" maintenance

**🔴 Intensive Practice:**
- List each with %
- Not confident yet
- Continue daily practice

---

### Step 3: Roadmap Review (5 min)

**Compare to Plan:**
- What did you plan for this week?
- What actually happened?
- Status: Ahead / On Track / Behind?
- Why the difference?

**Timeline Check:**
- Current milestone: X% complete
- Original estimate: [date]
- Updated estimate: [date]
- Difference: +/- X weeks
- Is this acceptable? (Usually yes!)

**Decision:**
- Continue as planned?
- Adjust timeline?
- Change approach?

**Remember:** Timeline is flexible! Adjust guilt-free based on reality.

---

### Step 4: Teaching Moment (5 min)

**Pick ONE thing you learned this week**

Write 3-5 sentences explaining it clearly, as if teaching someone who's never seen it.

**Requirements:**
- Must be clear and simple
- No jargon without explanation
- Include why it matters
- Real-world application

**Keep these!** They become great study material and portfolio content.

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### Don't Move to Maintenance Too Early
**Why:** Skills will fade if foundation isn't solid
**Fix:** Wait until YOU feel confident, not just performance
**Remember:** Better to spend extra time now than relearn later

### Don't Skip Challenge Mode
**Why:** Speed and confidence come from volume
**Fix:** Make "how many in 30 min?" a regular practice
**Benefit:** Builds automaticity faster

### Don't Have More Than 2 Topics in Intensive
**Why:** Scattered focus = slow progress on all
**Fix:** Max 2 topics, when one moves out, add new one
**Result:** Faster mastery, better retention

### Don't Wait a Week After Learning
**Why:** Forgetting gap makes intensive practice harder
**Fix:** Learn → NEXT DAY intensive practice
**Result:** Better retention, faster mastery

### Don't Ignore Your Feelings
**Why:** You know when you're truly confident
**Fix:** Honest self-assessment determines progress
**Trust:** Your gut feeling is usually right
